      Four steps in every mission

      1. 01

        Get started

        Read why the mission matters. Then follow the steps in HQ, or paste the Guide Me prompt into Gemini and let it walk you through them.

      2. 02

        Create it

        Every mission ends with something you’ll use, like a Gemini skill or an activity for students. HQ shows examples from your grade level or role.

      3. 03

        Verify it

        Check what Gemini made with the mission's test, such as a claims audit, before you use it.

      4. 04

        Completion note

        About two minutes in HQ: what you made, where it lives, and what you'd tell a colleague.

      Every mission follows the FPS AI Use Guide.

      Coaching all year

      You won’t work through the missions on your own. TJ and Andy coach every Champion from the first mission to the last.

      • One-on-one check-insMeet with TJ or Andy to plan, get unstuck or try an idea out loud.
      • Feedback on your missionsA coach reads every completion note and replies in HQ.
      • A mentor, if you want oneGet paired with a Cohort 1 Champion who has done the missions before.
      • Three reflectionsAfter Mission 1, after Mission 6 and after your third elective. Your coach reads them before your next check-in.

      Questions between check-ins? Email TJ or Andy anytime.
